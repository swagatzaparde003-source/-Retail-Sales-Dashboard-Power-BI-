# -Retail-Sales-Dashboard-Power-BI
📊 Project Overview

This project is an interactive Retail Sales Dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, and regional trends.

The dashboard transforms raw retail transaction data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations, KPI cards, slicers, and dynamic charts, helping businesses monitor performance and make data-driven decisions.

🎯 Key Objectives
Analyze overall retail sales performance
Track important business KPIs
Identify monthly and yearly sales trends
Compare sales performance across regions
Understand customer segmentation and age groups
Analyze product-wise sales performance
Compare different payment methods
Support data-driven business decision-making

💡 Key Insights
Sales performance remains relatively consistent across the major regions.
July 2025 recorded one of the highest monthly sales values.
Product-level analysis highlights Bread and Lipstick among the top-performing products shown in the dashboard.
Customer segments and age-group analysis provide insights into customer distribution and purchasing behavior.
Payment-method filtering allows users to interactively analyze sales based on Cash, Credit Card, and Debit Card transactions.
